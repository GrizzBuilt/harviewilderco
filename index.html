The Bedtime Riot

A few pages from Harvie the Tiny Punk Princess: The Bedtime Riot. Just enough to meet Harvie and get a taste of the bedtime rebellion.

Harvie was NOT sleepy.
Not even close.

Her little boots were still stompy.
Her bunny was still wide awake.

And her bedtime playlist still had THREE whole songs left.

“Tiny punk princesses NEVER sleep!”
she announced dramatically.

Pickles the Tone Gremlin dropped a guitar pick in shock.

Babu snored louder.
Pepé rolled onto his back like this was all very emotional.

Down the hallway she marched.

STOMP.
STOMP.
STOMP.

Her floppy bunny bounced under one arm.

Tonight…
there would be a BEDTIME RIOT.

Then suddenly—

YAWNNNNN.

Harvie froze.

That yawn came from HER.

Dad opened one sleepy eye.

“Rockstars gotta recharge too, kiddo.”

Harvie sighed.
“Even tiny punk princesses?”

Dad smiled.
“Especially tiny punk princesses.”

And that’s where we stop. The rest of Harvie’s bedtime riot is waiting in the book.
