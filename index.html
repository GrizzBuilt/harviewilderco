How do I choose the right fit?

These are requested size labels, not a measurement chart. Fits vary by blank. We’ll confirm the garment and measurements before payment; include fit questions in your notes. If between sizes, ask us before ordering.

When will my preorder ship?

This first drop is produced in small batches. Your production estimate and shipping window will be supplied with your quote before payment. No fixed ship date is promised at request stage. We’ll email you if timing changes.

How do payment and delivery work?

We review your request and email a final quote with available garments, flat $9 apparel shipping or Conway pickup with no shipping charge, and any applicable tax. Your order is confirmed only after you approve and pay the invoice. Lulu handles book purchases separately.

Can I change my request?

Reply to your confirmation email to discuss changes before payment. Any cancellation or return terms will be provided before payment so you can review them before your order is confirmed.

The Bedtime Riot

A few pages from Harvie the Tiny Punk Princess: The Bedtime Riot. Just enough to meet Harvie and get a taste of the bedtime rebellion.

Harvie was NOT sleepy.
Not even close.

Her little boots were still stompy.
Her bunny was still wide awake.

And her bedtime playlist still had THREE whole songs left.

“Tiny punk princesses NEVER sleep!”
she announced dramatically.

Pickles the Tone Gremlin dropped a guitar pick in shock.

Babu snored louder.
Pepé rolled onto his back like this was all very emotional.

Down the hallway she marched.

STOMP.
STOMP.
STOMP.

Her floppy bunny bounced under one arm.

Tonight…
there would be a BEDTIME RIOT.

Then suddenly—

YAWNNNNN.

Harvie froze.

That yawn came from HER.

Dad opened one sleepy eye.

“Rockstars gotta recharge too, kiddo.”

Harvie sighed.
“Even tiny punk princesses?”

Dad smiled.
“Especially tiny punk princesses.”

And that’s where we stop. The rest of Harvie’s bedtime riot is waiting in the book.
